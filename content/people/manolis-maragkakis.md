## Bio

Manolis is a Stadtman Principal Investigator in the Laboratory of Genetics and
Genomics at the National Institute on Aging (NIA), NIH.

His research investigates the high-dimensional regulation of gene expression as
a key component of discovering the mechanistic basis of human disease and aging.
His work merges experimental and computational methods to examine RNA stability
mechanisms, incorporating high-throughput sequencing and machine learning to map
regulatory networks across aging processes.
