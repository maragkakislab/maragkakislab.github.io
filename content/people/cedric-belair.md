## Research

Cedric works on RNA metabolism, contributing to projects on direct RNA
sequencing and transcriptome dynamics, including the development of the RNAkinet
deep learning framework for labeled RNA sequencing.

## Education & Training

- **Postdoctoral Associate** — Yale University School of Medicine (Wolin lab);
  RNA dynamics in human embryonic stem cells
- **Ph.D. Microbiology-Immunology** — University of Bordeaux; Regulation of microRNAs in gastric cells upon Helicobacter pylori infection
- **M.Sc. in Microbiology-Immunology** — University of Bordeaux; Development of RNA aptamers against the Hepatitis C Virus
