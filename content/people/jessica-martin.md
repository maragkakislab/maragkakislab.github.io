## Research

Jessica develops machine learning tools for the analysis of direct RNA
sequencing data. She led the development of RNAkinet, a deep neural network that
identifies newly synthesized RNA molecules labeled with 5-ethynyl uridine using
nanopore direct RNA sequencing.
