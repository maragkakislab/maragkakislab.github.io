## Summary

This study introduces a method for transcript-guided targeted enrichment of specific cell populations prior to single-nucleus RNA sequencing. By selecting nuclei based on expression of specific transcripts, the approach achieves deep profiling of rare cell types at scale, enabling discovery of cell-type-specific transcriptional programs that would be missed by unbiased approaches.
