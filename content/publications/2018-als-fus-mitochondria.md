## Summary

This study shows that disease-associated FUS mutations cause mitochondrial shortening and fragmentation in neurons, leading to neurotoxicity. The findings connect FUS dysfunction to mitochondrial dynamics, providing a potential mechanism for neurodegeneration in ALS patients carrying FUS mutations.
