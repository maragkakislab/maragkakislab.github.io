## Summary

This study identifies Topoisomerase 3β as a factor that facilitates piRNA biogenesis, with loss of TOP3B leading to reduced piRNA production, derepression of transposable elements, and impaired germ cell development. The findings reveal a novel role for a DNA/RNA topoisomerase in small RNA biology.
