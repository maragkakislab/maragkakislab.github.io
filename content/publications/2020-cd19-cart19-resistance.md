## Summary

This study identifies intron 2 retention in CD19 transcripts as a mechanism contributing to CAR-T19 therapy resistance in leukemias harboring subclonal frameshift mutations in CD19. The findings reveal how alternative splicing can undermine antigen-targeted cancer immunotherapy.
