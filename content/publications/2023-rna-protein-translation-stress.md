## Summary

This review covers the post-transcriptional mechanisms by which cells regulate protein synthesis in response to stress, focusing on the roles of RNA-binding proteins, non-coding RNAs, and mRNA modifications in controlling translation. The connections between stress-responsive translational control and aging are discussed.
