## Summary

This study reveals that 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (5hmC) enrichment in gene bodies acts as an epigenetic mechanism that constrains the amplitude of transcriptional changes occurring during aging. Genes with high 5hmC show attenuated age-related expression changes, identifying DNA hydroxymethylation as a regulator of transcriptional stability in aging tissues.
