## Summary

This study identifies a surveillance mechanism that monitors microRNA precursors and selectively degrades aberrant pre-miRNAs, ensuring the quality of miRNA biogenesis. The findings reveal a previously unknown layer of post-transcriptional regulation in the miRNA processing pathway.
