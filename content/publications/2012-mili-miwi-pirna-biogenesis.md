## Summary

Using CLIP-seq, this study maps the RNA targets of mouse PIWI proteins Mili and Miwi, providing insights into the mechanisms of piRNA biogenesis and the specific role of Miwi in regulating spermiogenesis through piRNA-mediated gene silencing.
