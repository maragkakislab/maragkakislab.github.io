## Summary

This study discovers ribothrypsis, a previously unknown mRNA decay process in which ribosomes trigger endonucleolytic cleavage of mRNAs in a ribosome-phased pattern. Ribothrypsis operates during active translation and represents a major pathway of canonical mRNA decay in human cells.
