## Summary

This study dissects the molecular interactions between Aubergine and Tudor-domain proteins and their roles in piRNA amplification through the ping-pong cycle and in the formation of the germ plasm. The findings provide mechanistic insight into how piRNA pathway components are organized in germ granules.
