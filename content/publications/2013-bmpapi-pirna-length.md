## Summary

This study identifies BmPAPI, a mitochondrial protein in the silkworm Bombyx mori, as a factor that modulates the length of mature piRNAs. The findings provide mechanistic insight into the piRNA 3' end processing pathway and link mitochondria to piRNA biogenesis.
