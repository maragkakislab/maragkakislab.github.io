## Summary

This study shows that piRNAs promote germ plasm localization of mRNAs through a sequence-dependent but not sequence-specific adhesion mechanism. The findings reveal that the overall sequence composition, rather than specific complementarity, determines which mRNAs are retained in the germ plasm.
